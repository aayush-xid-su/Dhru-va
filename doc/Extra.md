# Project Philosophy

Dhru-VA is designed with four primary goals:

- **Accuracy** – Reliable visualization of cellular telemetry.
- **Performance** – Fast rendering even with large datasets.
- **Scalability** – Easily extendable through modular architecture.
- **Accessibility** – Simple enough for students, powerful enough for RF engineers.

# Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Dhru-VA require a backend?

No. It can run entirely in the browser using simulated or locally stored data.

---

### Can it work with real hardware?

Yes. It is designed to integrate with devices such as ESP32, SIM7600, Android Telephony APIs, and compatible telemetry sources.

---

### Is it mobile friendly?

Yes. The interface is responsive and works on modern mobile browsers.

---

### Is it open for contributions?

Yes. Community contributions are welcome.
